# Pilot Scope — Ben Peretz Financial Protection Pilot

**Draft — review and adjust before sending.** Provider: CeoLyfestyle LLC
("Provider"). Client: Ben Peretz — Financial Protection & Planning ("Client").

## Objective

Deploy a customized instance of the AION Advisor Growth Engine for Client's
financial-protection practice: capture prospects through a branded Financial
Protection Checkup, qualify and score them, book appointments, and give Ben an
AI-assisted prep brief — with reporting on the whole funnel.

## What's included

**Configuration & branding**
- Branded Financial Protection Checkup (Client's name, logo, colors, bio)
- One configured sales pipeline and stage set
- Organization settings, roles, and scoring weights tuned for the practice

**Intelligence**
- AI lead qualification and deterministic lead scoring
- AI advisor preparation briefs (review-gated)
- Personalized educational results for prospects

**Automation (one configured set)**
- New-lead workflow (capture → qualify → score → route → notify)
- Appointment booking + reminder sequence
- One follow-up / nurture sequence using Client-approved messaging

**CRM & calendar**
- GoHighLevel configuration (pipelines, fields, contact/opportunity mapping)
- Booking calendar connection and confirmation flow

**Reporting**
- Executive dashboard (leads, qualification, appointments, pipeline value, conversion)

**Support**
- Setup and configuration
- One training/walkthrough session with Ben
- Email support during the pilot term

## What's not included (available in the Growth System or as add-ons)

- Multiple pipelines/campaigns and the referral engine
- Re-engagement and renewal workflows
- Advanced analytics/attribution and quarterly optimization
- Custom carrier/quote-platform integrations
- Paid ad management or content production

## Assumptions & dependencies

- Client provides the information listed in the close-package README.
- Client holds the required licenses for all states/products in scope.
- Client approves all disclosures, messaging, and compliance copy before go-live.
- GoHighLevel subscription is Client's (or provisioned with Client's authorization);
  third-party fees (GHL, messaging, etc.) are billed by those providers.

## Timeline

1. **Kickoff** (target Mon Aug 18, 2026) — intake of branding, licensing, messaging.
2. **Configuration** (~5–7 business days) — build the customized instance.
3. **Approval** — Client signs off on the 9-item checklist.
